        How to play

        • SteerHold the left or right edge of the screen. Keyboard: arrow keys or A and D.
        • HarpoonWhen the readout says LOCK, tap the centre of the screen or press Space. You reel in, then slingshot.
        • SlingshotYou phase through traffic and purge static while it burns. Chain harpoons to keep it alive.
        • BrakeHold both edges, or press the down arrow.
        • TimeNear misses, shards and slingshots add seconds. Reach the drop zone before the window closes.
        • StaticGlitching blocks cost three seconds and scramble your steering. A shield absorbs one hit.
